---

## Notes for Google Flow

1. **Character Consistency**: Keep the same person/avatar throughout all 6 scenes
2. **Branding**: Use consistent colors, fonts, and styling
3. **Pacing**: Each scene should have a clear beginning, middle, and end
4. **Vertical Format**: Remember 9:16 aspect ratio—no horizontal-oriented shots
5. **Length**: Exactly 10 seconds per scene
6. **Engagement**: Start strong and end with a hook or call-to-action

---

## Export Requirements

After generating scenes in Google Flow:
1. Export each scene as a separate video file (MP4 recommended)
2. Name files: `scene_1.mp4`, `scene_2.mp4`, etc.
3. Ensure consistent quality (resolution, bit rate)
4. Save to the project's `assets/` folder
5. Video should be ready for FFmpeg assembly
